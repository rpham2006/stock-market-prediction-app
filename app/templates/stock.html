Read this before you believe the number

  • Daily returns are mostly noise. At a one-day horizon, public equity markets are close to efficient. A model built from public price history should not be expected to beat them, and usually doesn't.
  • The band matters more than the point. A single price is false precision; the 80% band above is the real output.
  • The target date ignores market holidays. It is the next weekday, which occasionally lands on a closed session.
